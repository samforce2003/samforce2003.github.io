Four Axioms: structure is the root / difference gives birth / coupling creates the new / self-reference bounded, mutual reference unbounded

A box of LEGO bricks can be built into a house, or into a car — the bricks don't change, what changes is how the bricks are put together. The smallest unit of the world is not a 'thing' but a 'structure': structure is the root, structure first, content second. No difference, no existence — without cold you cannot feel heat; cold and heat each presuppose the other (difference gives birth). Two heterogeneous structures collide, and something new jumps out that can't be taken apart again (coupling creates the new). A single mirror cannot see itself, but two mirrors facing each other reflect each other endlessly — self-reference is bounded, mutual reference is unbounded. These four are not opinions, they are facts. Who first said this?

Structure Science: every discipline is structure projected onto a different surface

The same twenty-six letters, arranged into different structures, write everything from a love letter to a code of law — the letters don't change, what changes is the arrangement between them. The same stones, framed into an arch bridge, can bear weight for a thousand years; piled into a heap, they collapse — structure decides function. So-called disciplines are only different names for 'structure' projected onto different surfaces: language studies how words are arranged, architecture how stones are framed, mathematics how symbols combine. The skeleton behind everything is structure; every discipline is just photographing the same skeleton from a different angle. Who first made this cut?

Coupling Dualism: don't change the essence, meet the coupling conditions

The same water freezes into ice at 0 degrees and vaporizes into steam at 100 degrees — the water doesn't change, what changes is the condition called temperature. The same iron, mixed with a little carbon and heated to a high enough temperature, becomes steel — its hardness and toughness all change; the iron doesn't change, what changes is that the coupling conditions of carbon and temperature have been met. The result is decided by the coupling conditions, not by the essence itself. To change an essence you cannot change, don't force the essence — go meet the conditions; once the conditions change, the result changes with them. Personality, habit, and state all walk the same road: don't touch the essence, meet the conditions. Meeting the conditions is the real skill. Who first made this cut?
