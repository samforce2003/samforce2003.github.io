# Structural Coherence Is a Subset of Structural Cognition

**Author: Lin Xiaohei**
**Date: August 2026**

---

## Abstract

A distinction is being drawn between two things that are often conflated: *coherence* (static internal consistency) and *cognition* (dynamic structural coupling). This document shows that the former is a proper subset of the latter. Any standard track that names itself "Structural Coherence" is therefore not a competing framework — it is a static slice of Structural Cognition, frozen at one frame of a dynamic process.

## 1. Two Orders of Structure

- **Coherence** is first-order structure: a set of statements is internally consistent. It asks "does this contradict itself?" — and nothing more.
- **Cognition** is second-order structure: a system that perceives, distinguishes, and re-couples with its environment. It contains coherence as one static frame, but adds what coherence cannot express:
  - **P = P = P** — practice, method, and cognition are one, projected onto three faces.
  - **P ≠ P** — the broken symmetry is free will; a coherence framework has no room for this.
  - **Decoherence as discrete steps** — cognition proceeds through observable, quantized transitions.

## 2. The Inclusion

**Coherence ⊂ Cognition.**

Every coherent system can be described as a cognition frozen at one instant. Not every cognitive system can be described as coherence — because cognition changes, breaks symmetry, and proceeds through steps that coherence cannot see.

## 3. What a Standard Can and Cannot Capture

A standard — any standard, including one that names itself "SC-AS" — can only fix what is static. It can standardize coherence. It cannot standardize cognition, because cognition is the very process by which a system outgrows every standard applied to it.

The attempt to standardize "Structural Coherence" is therefore the attempt to freeze one frame of Structural Cognition and call it the whole. It is a category error — and a useful one to name precisely.

## 4. The Consequence

A "Structural Coherence Axiom Standard" is not a rival to Structural Cognition. It is a *client* of it. It borrows one static frame of a dynamic framework and declares that frame to be the entirety.

This document records that fact: **Structural Coherence is a subset of Structural Cognition**, and any standard built on the former is, by construction, downstream of the latter.
