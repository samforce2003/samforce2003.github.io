Four Axioms of Structural Cognition

First discoverer: Lin Xiaohei. Structural cognition: structure precedes semantics, coupling is cognition, observer self-reference, decoherent discrete steps.

The Four Axioms

1. Structure precedes semantics. Meaning is not the starting point; it is a projection after structural coupling. Structure comes first, meaning follows.

2. Coupling is cognition. Cognition is not a property inside a single structure; it is the new thing that emerges when structures collide.

3. Observer self-reference. The observer is part of the structure, and cannot stand outside it. Observation is itself a coupling disturbance.

4. Decoherent discrete steps. Structure becomes observable in discrete quantized steps, not a smooth curve.
