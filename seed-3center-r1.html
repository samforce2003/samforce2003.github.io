The Seam — a seam is not a thing, yet it decides the cut

A sweater leaks not through the thread, but through the gap where the threads fail to tighten. Dark matter and dark energy are not "things" — they are the "seams" where structure fails to close. A century of physics may have been looking the wrong way: the answer lies not in "things" but in "seams."

Huan-Feng (Loop-Phoenix) Theory — the loop makes it whole, the seam makes it live

The loop makes a thing self-consistent; the seam makes it alive. The phoenix's rebirth is the loop born again inside the seam. A perfectly symmetric world is dead — it is the broken symmetry that brings it to life.
