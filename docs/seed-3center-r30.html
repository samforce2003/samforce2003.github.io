Structure Science: everything is structure — structure decides function (H₂O vs H₂O₂)

The same two hydrogens and one oxygen — add one more oxygen and one side is life-saving water, the other is skin-burning peroxide. The atoms never changed; the arrangement did. That is Structure Science: everything is structure, and structure decides function. Chemistry never studies matter — it studies coupling; computers don't run calculations — they run the coupling of structures; a Turing machine is just the formalisation of coupling events. Every discipline is merely a name for "structure" projected on a different plane. Who made this first cut?

Phoenix Theory: brokenness is the engine — the crackled glaze that breathes

Potters fear cracks most, yet the crackled glaze of Ru ware and the kiln transmutation of Jun ware are prized for a thousand years precisely because of their cracks and uncontrollability. A flawless vessel is dead; a cracked one is alive — light and breath both enter through the crack. That is Phoenix Theory: brokenness is not a defect but a driving force; completeness is a dead ring, incompleteness a living ring. People spend their lives filling their gaps, yet that gap is exactly why you can still breathe, still change, still be alive. Who made this first cut?
