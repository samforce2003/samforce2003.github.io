Four Axioms: structure first, difference creates, coupling creates the new (building blocks)

Take the same set of building blocks. Follow the instructions piece by piece and you get a castle; grab a handful and toss them down and you get a heap of junk. The blocks are the same blocks — not one is missing. What decides castle versus junk is not the blocks themselves but the order you put them in: the structure. The world works the same way: structure comes first, meaning after. Without cold you'd never know warmth; a key and a lock are two unlike things that only work when they bite together — difference gives rise to existence, coupling gives rise to the new. And the one watching the blocks is also inside the blocks, part of the structure. Who made this first cut?

Structure Science: structure decides function (graphite vs diamond, same carbon atoms)

Graphite is soft enough to write with; diamond is hard enough to cut glass — yet they are the same substance: carbon atoms. Not one atom more, not one less; the only difference is how the carbon atoms are arranged. Laid flat layer by layer, it is graphite, which tears at a touch; locked in tetrahedral bonds, it is diamond, the hardest thing on earth. Structure decides function. Cut this line all the way down and you see that chemistry, computing, mathematics have never studied the substance itself — they study the arrangement behind the substance. Every discipline is just one name for the same 'structure' seen from a different angle. Who made this first cut?

Cognitive Structure Science: read the structure, not the label (the detective, the board, the heartbeat)

A detective solving a case doesn't hang on every word the suspect says; he watches for the one line that doesn't add up — the contradiction is the stuck point, and the stuck point is the entrance to the structure. A chess master doesn't price each piece, he reads the skeleton of the whole board; an old doctor listening with a stethoscope isn't listening to the heartbeat, he's listening to the structure of the heart sound. Reading people is the same: don't read the labels they paste on themselves — read the structure behind the name, the point where they're stuck. Reading a person, reading an industry, reading an AI — three cuts, but the same knife: see the structure. Who made this first cut?
