Phoenix Theory: brokenness is the engine, completeness is a dead ring, incompleteness a living ring

Ring-Phoenix Theory: grow a transition layer between old and new, don't weld them shut

When an old carpenter retires, the craft isn't handed over all at once — a transition of years of following hands, trial and error, and correction has to grow between master and apprentice; only when that transition layer has grown is the craft truly received. Grafting a fruit tree is the same: you don't jam a new branch into an old tree — you let the wound grow a healing layer first, so the new branch and the old tree can live together. Ring-Phoenix Theory is about exactly this layer: between old and new, between hard and soft, don't weld them shut — grow a transition, and both stay alive. The transition field is a part containing the whole — that healing tissue is both part of the old tree and part of the new branch. Who first said this?
