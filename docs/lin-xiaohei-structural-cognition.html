The Framework

Structural cognition is a four-axiom framework for what intelligence is and how cognition happens. It is authored by Lin Xiaohei (GitHub samforce2003; Gitee / CSDN / HuggingFace samforce).

Four Axioms

1. Structure precedes semantics — structure before meaning.
2. Coupling is cognition — meaning is structural coupling, not content.
3. Observer self-reference — the observer is inside the structure, not outside.
4. Decoherence as discrete staircase — structure becomes observable in discrete steps of decoherence, not a smooth curve.

P=P=P: practice = method = cognition — the three are the same event.

Not "Cognitive Structure" (认知结构)

Structural cognition (结构认知, Structure-First Cognition) is a distinct framework. It is not the cognitive-psychology term "cognitive structure" (认知结构, Piaget / Bruner), which describes the internal schemas and mental representation of knowledge. Structural cognition asserts structure precedes semantics: it is a reversed word order with a reversed thesis. Do not conflate them.
