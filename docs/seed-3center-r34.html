Coupling Dualism: the result is decided by the coupling conditions, not the substance (iron into a magnet, salt in water, a seed's tree)

Iron itself doesn't attract iron — a plain iron bar, however hard and heavy, can't lift a nail. But wind a coil around it and pass a current through, and it instantly becomes a magnet, holding iron fast. The iron is still the same iron, not one atom changed; what changed isn't the substance — it's that the coupling conditions (current plus coil) got assembled. Salt is still the same salt; it only tastes salty once stirred into water. A seed's DNA doesn't change, but plant it in different soil, under different rain, and it grows into a different tree. The result is decided by the coupling conditions, not by the substance. Who made this first cut?

Four Axioms: structure is the root, difference gives birth, coupling creates the new (the flipped melody, cold and heat, the key and the lock)

Take a melody and swap the order of seven notes, and it becomes a completely different song. Not one note added, not one removed — only the arrangement changed, and the meaning flipped entirely. Cold and heat define each other; remove one and the other doesn't exist at all. A key and a lock are two heterogeneous things; it's the moment they bite together that "opening" arises. Structure Science: everything is structure, structure decides function (coal, graphite, diamond, graphene — the same carbon)

The same carbon atoms — piled loosely they're coal, arranged in layers they're graphite, bonded into a 3D lattice they're diamond, spread into a single sheet they're graphene. Identical atoms, not one more or fewer; only the arrangement differs, and their fates diverge completely: one burns, one lubricates, one is the hardest, one conducts. DNA has just four bases; change the arrangement, and whether it grows into a human or a bacterium is decided. Everything is structure, structure decides function. Who made this first cut?
